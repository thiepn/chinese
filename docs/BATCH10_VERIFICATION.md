## Implemented

- Connected guided practice sessions now combine all 12 existing contextual word-sense cases, 24 exact-form Chinese sentence tasks and 30 dialogue mission-stage configurations, along with the previously available six demo drill types and reading exercises. 5/10/20-question session limits and balance/weakness/connected/reading/pronunciation focus are supported.
- Existing `senseEvents`, `sentenceEvents`, `missionEvents`, `drillEvents` and `readingEvents` are reused. Supplemental results cannot mutate scheduled `cards` or `events` or mark a lesson learned. All 74 demo character lessons, 12 mini-readings and 10 dialogues are retained.
- Persistent assisted-hint flag prevents answer hints from later receiving independent credit after route changes or app restarts. Saved guided feedback and session checkpoints remain compatible with older sessions that do not have the new optional flag.
- Expanded written-tone guidance, with syllable contours, tone-error and syllable-error remediation, and explicit no-acoustic-assessment disclaimer; pronunciation audio remains device speech synthesis without qualification.
- Today and Progress surface contextual skill suggestions and separate contextual attempt analytics; contextual exercises link back to teaching screens, and conversation passages link to written-form matches in the original demo vocabulary index.
- Due-only review now diversifies consecutive learning targets by skill and lesson where possible and surfaces queue-priority reasons without revealing answers.
- Responsive CSS improvements for learning bridges, guided exercises, written-tone cues and small screens; no claim of final visual-direction approval.
