## Source families and coverage limits

- Original target 1: historical foundations and P31, original P25/P32 grammar, P34 reading and P33/P34 spoken scripts via device TTS.
- Original target 2: P35 character/vocabulary, P36 grammar, P38 reading and P37 dialogue.
- Original target 3: P39 character/vocabulary, P40 grammar, P41 reading and P42/P44/P45 spoken scripts.
- Original target 4: P46/P47 character/vocabulary and grammar, P46/P47/P48 reading and P46/P47/P48/P49 dialogue.
- Original target 5: P50/P51 character/vocabulary and grammar, P50/P51 reading and P50/P51 dialogue.
- Original target 6: five P71 advanced original reading dossiers, P75/P77 consequence dialogues and band-6 independent tasks. No dedicated level-6 character/grammar inventory exists.
- Original target 7–9: twelve existing original advanced independent missions from the shared band; there is no separately verified level-7, level-8 or level-9 corpus.
- The source labels are editorial **difficulty targets only**. Authenticity of Mandarin, tone-marked Pinyin, bilingual alignment, teaching fairness and rights remains subject to human native-speaker/editorial review.
- Listening is original **scripted** Chinese read with device speech synthesis. It is *not* a licensed human recording library, native listening-rate assessment, speech recognition, voice capture or an official listening exam. Do not infer oral/translation qualification from these activities.
- Reading/comprehension answer records are exercises, not independent certification; the original review and student state authorities remain unchanged.

## Completed bounded product repair

The original HSK level chooser previously showed mostly character lessons, without connecting them to the existing grammar, reading or scripted dialogue sources. For each target with actual source-backed material, the selected-level screen now shows direct grammar, reading and listening/dialogue links using their immutable native IDs, routes and grading engines. The original level-6 reading preview stays singular rather than duplicating the same five sources, and level 7–9 continues to show existing missions only. The app does not create a shadow course, new learner state, target-credit event or unlicensed content.

Test contract: `tests/c9-hsk-authentic-skill-sources.test.mjs`, `scripts/c9_hsk_skill_source_acceptance.py`, `scripts/hsk3_source_coverage.mjs`, original full Node/Chromium regression and offline/cache migration. No manual/billable CI dispatched.
